SDG Initiatives: Goals 1, 2, and 3

SDG 1: No Poverty

The objective of SDG 1 is to eradicate extreme poverty through inclusive economic growth, social protection systems, and equitable access to essential resources.

  • Strengthening social protection frameworks for vulnerable populations.
  • Expanding equitable access to education, financial services, and employment opportunities.
  • Building communities’ resilience against economic and climate-related shocks.

SDG 2: Zero Hunger

SDG 2 promotes global food security by supporting sustainable agriculture, improving nutrition, and ensuring reliable food systems that can withstand climate challenges.

  • Enhancing agricultural productivity through sustainable and climate-smart techniques.
  • Improving access to nutritious foods across all socioeconomic groups.
  • Supporting smallholder farmers with training, technology, and fair market access.

SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being

SDG 3 aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for people of all ages by strengthening healthcare systems and reducing global health risks.

  • Expanding availability of quality healthcare, medicines, and preventive services.
  • Reducing maternal and child mortality through improved medical support.
  • Enhancing mental health care, disease prevention, and environmental health measures.
