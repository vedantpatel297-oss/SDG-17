Collaborating for Change: Volunteer Groups on SDGs 1, 2, and 3

Introduction

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) aim to make the world a better place. SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 2 (Zero Hunger), and SDG 3 (Good Health) focus on helping people live safe, healthy, and happy lives.

Volunteers help these goals succeed by supporting communities and improving lives.

SDG 1: No Poverty

Mission

To support families and communities so they can escape poverty.

Activities

  • Training on money management
  • Helping small businesses grow
  • Emergency help for families in need

Testimonials

“The training helped me start my own shop.” — Maria
“Helping others gives me purpose.” — Adeel

SDG 2: Zero Hunger

Mission

To make sure every person has access to healthy food.

Activities

  • Community gardens
  • Sharing extra food to avoid waste
  • Teaching communities about nutrition

Testimonials

“The garden helps feed my family every week.” — Fatima
“Helping others eat healthy makes me proud.” — Jared

SDG 3: Good Health

Mission

To help communities stay healthy through clinics and support groups.

Activities

  • Mobile clinics for checkups
  • Mental health support groups
  • School health and hygiene lessons

Testimonials

“The clinic saved my son’s life.” — Samuel
“The support circles help people feel heard.” — Leila
