SDG Events: Goal 1 & Goal 3

SDG 1: No Poverty – Event

Education & Employment Awareness Drive
📅 Date: 14 January 2026
📍 Venue: Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC), Mumbai

This event spreads awareness about the importance of education and job opportunities in improving life. It also provides free primary education for children in need.

  • Awareness sessions on how education shapes the future.
  • Guidance about jobs and skill development.
  • Offering free primary education to underprivileged children.
  • Distributing basic educational materials.

SDG 3: Good Health & Well-Being – Event

Free Health Check-Up & Rural Vaccination Awareness Camp
📅 Date: 3 March 2026
📍 Venue: Goregaon Exhibition Centre, Mumbai

This event provides free health check-ups and teaches rural communities why taking vaccines on time is important for a healthy life.

  • Free medical check-ups for everyone.
  • Teaching rural people the importance of regular vaccines.
  • Vaccination support for children and elders.
  • Free health booklets and doctor advice.
