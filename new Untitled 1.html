The 17 Sustainable Development Goals

← Back to Main Page

SDG 1: No Poverty

End poverty so everyone can have a better life.

SDG 2: Zero Hunger

Make sure everyone has enough safe and healthy food.

SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being

Help all people stay healthy and live well.

SDG 4: Quality Education

Give everyone access to good and equal education.

SDG 5: Gender Equality

Ensure equal rights and opportunities for women and men.

SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation

Provide clean water and safe toilets for all people.

SDG 7: Affordable and Clean Energy

Give people access to clean and low-cost energy.

SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth

Create good jobs and support a growing economy.

SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Build strong roads, buildings, and encourage new ideas.

SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

Reduce unfair differences between people and countries.

SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Make cities safe, clean, and good places to live.

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

Use resources wisely and reduce waste.

SDG 13: Climate Action

Take action to protect the planet from climate change.

SDG 14: Life Below Water

Protect the oceans and the animals living in them.

SDG 15: Life on Land

Care for forests, plants, and animals on land.

SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Promote peace, fairness, and strong governments.

SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals

Work together globally to achieve all the goals.
